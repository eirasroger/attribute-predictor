    What it found, and what it made of it

      The record

      Nobody writes a composition the same way twice.

      This is where reading stops being pattern matching. The same board, declared four ways: a table, a sentence, another language, and a different basis entirely. All four have to land in the same shape, or none of them can be compared to anything.

        Normalised

          Total 100,0

          A composition is not one value. It is a list of materials and the share of each, and a share means nothing until you know what it is a share of. Getting the basis wrong is how a whole catalogue quietly stops adding up.

          One product. Four documents. One row.

          No single document holds the whole picture. The declaration has the carbon, the data sheet has the physics, the end of life sheet has the part everyone forgets to ask for. Read together they make one record.

            What comes back

            Every value points back.

            A number with no source is a number somebody has to go and check by hand. Each field carries the document it came from and the place inside it, so the value and the evidence arrive together.

            Empty is an answer.

            When a document does not say, the field comes back empty and names the document it looked in. A blank you can trust is worth more than a value you cannot.
