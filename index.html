A declaration describes a product that exists.
Many of the decisions that shape it happen first.

Where it fits.

Two moments in a project share the same problem: a decision worth quantifying, and nothing yet to quantify it with.

  • Early design

    Compare compositions in minutes, while everything is still open. Iterate on the option, not on the paperwork.

  • Renovation and existing stock

    The partition already in the building has no declaration and never will. Until now that was a blank. Now it is a range.

A full LCA does the accounting once the design settles and verified data exists. Attribute Predictor covers the stretch before that, where the decisions are still being made.

      Estimated, per kilogram

        Measured against its own kind.

        A raw value per kilogram tells you very little. Heavy materials score well simply by being heavy. So every result is placed against the real spread for that category: the median, and the band the middle half of products fall inside.

        It says how sure it is.

        A verdict reads better, typical or worse than typical. Every estimate also carries its own error range, and when that range crosses the threshold the wording softens to likely and the colour eases off.

        Better than typical The error range sits clear of the threshold.
        Likely better than typical The range crosses it. The call is still open.

        Then open one up.

        A total tells you the size of the number. The stages tell you where it comes from. Every indicator is estimated stage by stage, and each stage carries its own certainty.

        A1 to A3, C3, C4 and D follow from the materials themselves, so they can be estimated with an uncertainty the model knows and states. A4 to A5 and C1 to C2 are set by transport distance and site process, which belong to the project. Those stay outside the model, and outside the claim.

        Accuracy, stated as a factor.

        These indicators span six orders of magnitude, so accuracy is reported as fold error: how close the estimate lands, as a multiple.

        1.07–1.39× Median fold error, across targets
        2–6× p90 fold error
        ~8 500 Labelled products behind it

        It runs on your machine.

        A Windows desktop application. Changing a material updates all twenty-five estimates in about five milliseconds, fast enough to keep pace with the conversation you are having about the design.

        Inference is entirely offline. No account, no upload, no telemetry. The compositions you are still arguing about stay on the laptop.

        Predicts. Estimates.
        And tells you how far to trust it.
